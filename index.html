            Add it to your phone or computer

            Open it like an app, straight from your home screen.

            Use this link to install it with its proper icon: https://harleyloft47-king.github.io/daily-checklist/ Open it in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android), then follow the steps below.
            1. Open this page in Safari.
            2. Tap the Share button ⬆︎ at the bottom of the screen (on iPad it's at the top).
            3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
            4. Tap Add. The checklist icon appears on your home screen.
            1. Open this page in Chrome.
            2. Tap the menu ⋮ in the top-right corner.
            3. Tap Add to Home screen (it may say Install app).
            4. Tap Add or Install. The checklist icon appears on your home screen.
            1. Open this page in Chrome or Edge.
            2. Click the menu ⋮ (Chrome) or ⋯ (Edge) in the top-right corner.
            3. In Chrome, choose Cast, save and share → Install page as app. In Edge, choose Apps → Install this site as an app.
            4. Click Install. It opens in its own window and shows up in your Start menu.
            1. Open this page in Safari.
            2. In the menu bar, choose File → Add to Dock.
            3. Click Add. The checklist icon appears in your Dock.

            Using Chrome on a Mac? Follow the Windows steps instead.

            Your checkmarks and progress are saved on each device separately.

            Checkmarks clear every morning. Your task list stays.
